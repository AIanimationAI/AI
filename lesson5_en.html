Lesson 5: Music

Compose the soundtrack for your film. Explore how music sets the mood, drives the rhythm and enhances every scene.

5.1 Why Music Matters

Music is the emotional backbone of your animation. A cheerful melody can make a scene feel playful, while a slow piano can make it feel sad or thoughtful. Choosing the right music helps your audience connect with the story.

5.2 Exploring Suno

Suno is an AI music generator that creates songs and instrumental tracks based on text prompts. It supports different genres and moods, lets you extend your tracks and even extract individual instruments (stems)【47503203825247†L177-L197】.

Writing Music Prompts

Describe the mood, genre and instruments you want. For example: “An upbeat pop song with acoustic guitar and drums, cheerful and energetic” or “A calm ambient track with piano and soft strings, perfect for a sunset scene”. Mention tempo (fast or slow) and whether you want lyrics or instrumental.

5.3 Composing Your Track

Start by deciding on the mood for your scene. Is it a chase, a celebration or a sad goodbye? Write a short prompt and generate a track in Suno. Listen carefully and adjust the prompt until the music feels right.

Tips for Great Music
  • Keep it simple: avoid too many instruments or busy rhythms that distract from the story.
  • Match the tempo to the action: fast music for excitement, slow music for calm moments.
  • Use repetition: repeating themes or motifs ties scenes together.

5.4 Covers & Adaptation

Suno can create cover versions of existing songs in new styles or languages. You can also adapt a generated track by changing instruments or slowing it down. This flexibility helps you fine‑tune the sound to match your visuals.

5.5 Synchronizing Music & Animation

When editing, align key moments in your video with beats in the music. For example, sync a character jump with a drum hit. If a scene slows down, fade the music gently. CapCut makes it easy to trim and reposition audio clips to fit your footage【598944673568178†L130-L176】.

Example Sync Plan

Scene of Lucy launching her machine: use an uplifting crescendo as she takes off. When she reaches the lighthouse, let the music pause for a moment to build tension, then bring in a triumphant melody as the light turns on.

5.6 Assignment

  1. Create a 30‑second music track for one of your scenes using Suno. Describe the mood, genre and instruments in your prompt.
  2. Generate a second version with a different mood (e.g., adventurous vs. calm) and compare which fits better.
  3. Experiment with syncing the track to your animated clips. Note where beats align with actions.
