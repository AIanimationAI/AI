Lesson 1: Introduction to AI & Animation

Discover how AI can help you create vibrant animations and why every great film starts with a simple story.

1.1 Understanding AI Animation

Artificial intelligence is changing the way we create cartoons and films. Instead of drawing every frame by hand, you can use AI tools to generate characters, backgrounds and even whole scenes from text descriptions. This lets you focus on what matters most: storytelling and creativity.

For example, the first AI‑generated cartoon followed a little locomotive named Tino. The creators didn’t start with fancy software — they brainstormed a story and characters, then wrote detailed prompts to generate the images【360697135777347†L28-L44】. Here is what one of their prompts looked like:

A cute, colorful little train named Tino is at a small, charming station, illustrated in a vibrant 2D cartoon style. He has expressive, oversized eyes and a big smile. Friendly cartoon animals like rabbits and birds wave goodbye. The sun is shining in a clear blue sky with whimsical flowers blooming along the tracks.【360697135777347†L40-L50】

Notice how the prompt describes the subject (Tino the train), the environment (a station with animals and flowers) and the style (vibrant 2D). Such detail helps the AI understand what you want to see. Once the images were ready, the team animated them into short clips and assembled the scenes into a story. Music was added last to set the emotional tone【360697135777347†L52-L78】.

1.2 Why Story Comes First

Even with powerful tools, animation begins with an idea. Ask yourself: Who is my protagonist? Where does the action take place? What happens first, next and last? A storyboard is a series of panels that outline your plot visually. It helps you organize your thoughts and plan each shot.

How to Create a Storyboard

Start with a blank sheet of paper and draw boxes for each scene. In each box, sketch the main action and write a sentence describing what’s happening. You don’t need to be an artist — stick figures are fine! The goal is to map out the flow of your story before you generate images.

1.3 Writing Effective Prompts

Prompts are the instructions you give an AI image generator. Good prompts usually include three parts:

  • Subject: Who or what is the focus? (e.g., “a brave cat”, “a futuristic city”)
  • Context: Where is it happening? What’s the environment? (e.g., “inside a floating spaceship”, “on a sunny beach”)
  • Style & Mood: How should it look and feel? (e.g., “in the style of a hand‑drawn cartoon”, “bright and cheerful”)

Here is a comparison between a vague and a descriptive prompt:

Vague prompt: 
A boy rides a bike.
Descriptive prompt: 
A smiling teenage boy wearing a red hoodie rides a blue bicycle down a tree‑lined street on a sunny spring morning, illustrated in a vibrant anime style.
Tips for Great Prompts
  • Be specific about colors, emotions and actions.
  • Mention any props or background elements that are important to the scene.
  • Specify the art style (cartoon, anime, watercolor, 3D) and mood (cheerful, mysterious, dramatic).

1.4 Your First Assignment

Ready to begin? Follow these steps:

  1. Write a short story idea in one paragraph. Introduce the main character, the setting and the conflict.
  2. Create a storyboard with 4–6 panels. Sketch each scene and jot down what happens.
  3. Write a descriptive prompt for each panel using the guidelines above. Include subject, context, style and mood.
