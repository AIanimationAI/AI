Lesson 4: Voice & Sound

Give your characters a voice and fill your world with sound. Learn how intonation, narration and effects bring stories to life.

4.1 Intonation & Expression

How a line is spoken changes everything. Intonation conveys emotion, personality and meaning. Try saying “I can’t believe it” in a happy, sad and angry tone — they all feel different!

Common Voice Emotions
  • Joyful: upbeat, light and fast.
  • Sad: slower, softer and lower pitch.
  • Angry: louder, sharper and clipped.
  • Surprised: rising pitch and quick.

4.2 Writing Your Script

Before recording, write what each character will say. Keep sentences short and natural. Stage directions (like “whispers” or “shouts”) help actors or AI know how to deliver the line.

Script Example 
Lucy (excited): I did it! The machine is ready!
Storm (rumbling): *Thunder booms in the distance*
Lucy (whispers): I hope this works…

4.4 Recording Your Own Voice

If you want a personal touch, record yourself! Find a quiet room, use a smartphone or microphone, and speak clearly. Rehearse the line to capture the right emotion. You can mix your recording with AI voices if you have multiple characters.

4.5 Sound Effects & Ambience

Sound effects (like footsteps, wind or thunder) and background ambience (crowds, forests, cities) make your scenes feel real. You can find free sound libraries online or generate them with tools like Suno or CapCut. Layer sounds gently so they don’t overpower the dialogue.

4.6 Communicating With AI

When generating voices or sounds with AI, describe exactly what you want. Mention the speaker’s age, accent, mood and pace. For example: “A friendly young girl speaks in a soft whisper with a hint of excitement.” The clearer your description, the better the result.

4.7 Assignment

  1. Write a script for your story. Include dialogue tags (who says what) and stage directions.
  2. Record one sentence three times: happy, sad and angry. Listen to how the mood changes.
  3. Choose a voice for each character using ElevenLabs or another text‑to‑speech tool, and generate their lines.
  4. Collect or create sound effects for important actions in your story.
